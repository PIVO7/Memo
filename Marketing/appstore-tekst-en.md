## Description (max 4000 tekens)

> Memo! is the classic memory game, made for kids and the whole family. Take turns flipping two cards. Are they the same? Then you've found a pair and you go again right away!
>
> TOGETHER ON ONE DEVICE
> Play against each other on a single iPhone or iPad. The device goes from hand to hand, and the game keeps track of whose turn it is and who has the most pairs.
>
> OR AGAINST THE COMPUTER
> Three opponents, each with their own character: Dommel forgets almost everything, Robbie remembers quite a lot and Professor Punt never forgets a thing. And they play fair: the computer never peeks under the cards, it remembers (and forgets!) just like a person. So the game grows along with your child.
>
> AGAINST THE CLOCK
> Playing alone? Find all the pairs as fast as you can and try to beat your own time.
>
> LEARNING WITHOUT LESSONS
> New players get a short guided tour, and the "How does Memo work?" rules page is always close by. Three board sizes, from 6 pairs for the youngest to 12 pairs for true memory champions. Meanwhile there's plenty of remembering and comparing going on — but we just call it playing.
>
> MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
> Big cards, cheerful colors, a personal avatar for every player and confetti for the winner. Play straight away as a guest, or with your own profile that keeps track of trophies and records.
>
> SAFE AND CALM
> No ads, no accounts, no internet required. Everything stays on your own device. Period.
>
> START FREE, UNLOCK ONCE
> For free you play together on one device or solo against Robbie, in the classic theme. The one-time Family Version unlocks Against the clock, all three opponents, the Candy, Ocean and Night color themes, and per-player statistics — with trophies, records and win streaks. One purchase for the whole family, through Family Sharing. And it sits behind a parental gate, so little fingers can't buy anything by accident.
>
> Ready to find them all? Memo!
